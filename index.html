About me

I was born in Syria and had a simple and happy childhood. When I was four years old, my family moved to Saudi Arabia. One of my favorite childhood memories is our summer road trips between Damascus and Riyadh.

The journey took around 24 hours. My father drove the car, my mother sat next to him, and my five siblings and I sat in the back. We spent the long journey laughing, playing games, telling jokes and listening to music when we got tired.

Even simple food tasted special during those trips. A cheese or mortadella sandwich felt different when we ate it together on the road. These journeys are still some of my happiest childhood memories.
